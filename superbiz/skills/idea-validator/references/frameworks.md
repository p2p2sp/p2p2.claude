# Frameworks used

Only links that were verified when this skill was written (2026-09-03) are included. Where no canonical source could be verified, the framework is described without a link and marked as such. Re-verify before citing in a report; put the verification date in the report appendix.

## Lean Canvas (Ash Maurya)
One-page business model: problem, segments, UVP, solution, channels, revenue, costs, key metrics, unfair advantage. Used in step 1.
- LEANSTACK (creator's site): https://leanstack.com
- Author's writing on product/market fit measurement: https://medium.com/lean-stack/how-i-am-measuring-product-market-fit-12db33ef9219

## The Mom Test (Rob Fitzpatrick)
Customer interviews that ask about past behaviour, not opinions about your idea. Used for the interview experiment and for the Customer member's stance.
- Publisher page (revised edition): https://www.simonandschuster.com/books/The-Mom-Test/Rob-Fitzpatrick/9798893312560

## Sean Ellis product/market fit survey
"How would you feel if you could no longer use this product?" — 40% "very disappointed" is the widely cited benchmark. Necessary, not sufficient; false positives are documented.
- Described by Ash Maurya: https://medium.com/lean-stack/the-first-thing-that-matters-product-market-fit-a7cab485b003
- Critique / false positives: https://kromatic.com/blog/false-positives-and-product-market-fit/

## Lean Startup (Eric Ries) — build/measure/learn
Iterative validation loop; the reason the process ends with experiments and pre-set thresholds. **No official URL verified in this session** — cite the book by title and author only.

## TAM / SAM / SOM
Total / serviceable / obtainable market. No single canonical source; treat as a method, not a citation. The skill requires the method (top-down or bottom-up) to be shown next to every figure, which matters more than a reference.

## Desirability / feasibility / viability
Risk categorisation used in step 2 (commonly attributed to design-thinking practice at IDEO). **No specific URL verified in this session.**
